# A gift for Anisha 💜 — v3

A single `index.html` — her photo is embedded in the file, music streams from Archive.org. No build step.

## The idea
She's a BTS fan (💜), so the whole page is purple: 7 hearts to light, the Magic Shop, and "Borahae" at the end. Her own line — some things make your heart smile every day — is woven into the Magic Shop letter, in my own words.

## Music — v2 (2026-11-11)
BTS "Magic Shop" — the actual BTS song, not a cover (Archive.org item bts_20210519, plain MP3, 4:36, no ads, NOT YouTube — YouTube inserts ads). Plays softly throughout, starting on the first tap (volume 0.10, ducks on firework booms). The sound toggle pauses/resumes it.

## Message — v3 (2026-11-11, his exact wording, approved before pushing)
All about her, almost no "I" anywhere. No together / not-together wording at all, no "I want to impress you", no "brother" (just "family"), no "dreams in Los Angeles". The Magic Shop letter: she is cute and innocent, her family was so welcoming, their house felt like home. The final card: the Colorado days, the food her family made, the laughter at her house; she loves BTS videos, already saw them live once, may she get many more chances — her future is bright; Happy Tihar, and her dream of seeing BTS live again.

## The journey — one screen leads to the next
1. **Envelope** — "For Anisha" — tap anywhere to open, with a chime
2. **Seven hearts** — tap each purple heart (Smile / Kindness / Courage / Dreams / Warmth / Grace / Light). Light all 7 and the mandala blooms, then Next unlocks
3. **The Magic Shop** — tap the door 🚪 and a letter appears inside
4. **Fireworks** — tap the sky for purple fireworks, with boom + crackle sounds
5. **Her photo** — her Dashain photo, pink sari
6. **Gift card** — "For Anisha, with all my heart" — particle text, a letter, replay button

No Instagram links anywhere. No name signed. Mobile-friendly.
